Reflex Lab: Privacy Policy

Publisher: Startup180 · Effective: 18 November 2026

Summary: Reflex Lab does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and makes no network connections.

What the app stores

To show your personal bests and recent reaction-time trend, the app saves a small file (profile.json) in its private storage on your headset. It contains only:

DataPurpose
Best score per drillShown on the drill cards and results screen
Best and last 10 reaction times (milliseconds) per drillPersonal-best tracking and the daily "readiness" comparison
Number of sessions per drillDetecting your first run of a drill

This file never leaves your device. It contains no name, account, device identifier, location, voice, image, or body or movement recordings.

What the app does not do

  • It does not request internet access and cannot send data anywhere.
  • It does not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • It does not use the microphone, cameras, passthrough, eye, face or hand-tracking data.
  • Controller and headset position are used in real time to run the game and are never stored.

Meta platform data

Your headset's operating system and the Meta Horizon Store process data under Meta's own policies (for example purchases and app usage). Reflex Lab does not receive or access that data. See Meta's Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Reflex Lab permanently deletes all of its data from your headset. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing held on our side to delete. You can still contact us with any request.

Children

Reflex Lab does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app's data practices change (for example, if online leaderboards are added), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
